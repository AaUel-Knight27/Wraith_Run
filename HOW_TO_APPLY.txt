Ashfields ruined-look pass 1
1. Copy the folders in this zip over your Godot project (same paths, overwrite when asked).
   If your local project has diverged from GitHub, diff scenes/maps/Ashfields.tscn and
   mapkit/Outdoor/OutdoorMapBuilder.cs by hand instead of overwriting.
2. Open the project in Godot, let it build the C# and import the new files.
3. Open scenes/maps/Ashfields.tscn, select the Builder node, press "Build Map" in the inspector.
   (The map's terrain and props are baked, so nothing changes until you rebuild.)
4. In the Output panel look for: "OutdoorMapBuilder: placed N/16 craters."
