# Prison material sources

All textures below are from Poly Haven and are released under CC0. The downloaded 1K JPEG maps are used for albedo, OpenGL normal, roughness, and ambient occlusion.

- [Concrete Wall 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_001) — bare concrete and plaster walls.
- [Painted Concrete](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_concrete) — worn green painted walls.
- [Concrete Floor Worn 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001) — prison floors.
- [Rusty Metal 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_02) — cell bars, fixtures, and metal props.
- [Dirty Tiles](https://polyhaven.com/a/dirty_tiles) — shower and washroom surfaces.
