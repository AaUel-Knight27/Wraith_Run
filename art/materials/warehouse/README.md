# Warehouse surface materials

The warehouse uses the 1K JPG PBR maps from ambientCG:

- Corrugated Steel 009: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=CorrugatedSteel009
- Concrete 034: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034

Both assets are released under Creative Commons CC0. The source ZIPs are available in ambientCG's 1K-JPG downloads. The project keeps the color, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps needed by the Godot materials; AO and displacement maps are omitted.
